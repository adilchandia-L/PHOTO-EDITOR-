How to use

Choose a photo, then pick a tab on the right. Adjust changes brightness, contrast and colour. Enhance improves the photo in one tap. Filters give a quick look. Crop picks a shape or trims edges. Text and Stickers add words and emojis where you choose. Effects adds borders, dark corners or grain. Background removes the background. Use Undo if you change your mind, and Download when you're done.

Why is background removal slow the first time?

The AI model has to download once. After that it is kept in your browser's cache and runs faster.

Why does a sticker or text look too small?

Increase the size slider. Sizes are a percentage of the picture, so bigger photos need bigger numbers.

Does it upload my photo?

No. Editing happens in your browser. The photo is not stored after you close the page.
